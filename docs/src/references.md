# References

## Primary Reference

- W. Sun, Q. Fu, and Z. Chen, *T-matrix Method for Light Scattering by Nonspherical Particles*, Chapter 3 in "Light Scattering Reviews" (2020).
  The conventions, notation, and equation numbering used throughout TMatrix.jl follow this reference.

## Mie Scattering

- G. Mie, "Beiträge zur Optik trüber Medien, speziell kolloidaler Metallösungen," *Annalen der Physik*, 330(3), 377–445 (1908).
- C. F. Bohren and D. R. Huffman, *Absorption and Scattering of Light by Small Particles*, Wiley (1983).

## T-matrix Methods

- M. I. Mishchenko, L. D. Travis, and D. W. Mackowski, "T-matrix computations of light scattering by nonspherical particles: A review," *J. Quant. Spectrosc. Radiat. Transfer*, 55(5), 535–575 (1996).
- M. I. Mishchenko, G. Videen, V. A. Babenko, N. G. Khlebtsov, and T. Wriedt, "T-matrix theory of electromagnetic scattering by particles and its applications: a comprehensive reference database," *J. Quant. Spectrosc. Radiat. Transfer*, 88, 357–406 (2004).

## Julia Packages

- `SpecialFunctions.jl` — Spherical Bessel functions
- `LegendrePolynomials.jl` — Associated Legendre polynomials
- `WignerSymbols.jl` — Exact rational Wigner 3-j symbols
- `FastGaussQuadrature.jl` — Gauss-Legendre quadrature
- `StaticArrays.jl` — Fixed-size arrays for small vectors
